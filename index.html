MetricTargetWhy it matters for you
Calories~2,900 kcal~500 under maintenance → ~0.5 kg/wk fat loss. The deficit is what reveals the physique.
Protein240 gProtects muscle in the deficit — the single most important macro on a cut.
Carbs~300 gFuels training; put most around your session. Flexes with calories.

These run correlations on however many days are currently logged - early on that's a small n, so treat single-day-food → next-day-metric links as leads to watch, not proven effects. Running many pairs at once means a few will cross the significance line by chance alone (multiple comparisons) - the "notable links" table below flags that explicitly. The weekly and body-composition views have even fewer points and are directional/descriptive, not statistically powered. Recomputed fresh from your logged data every time you publish.
